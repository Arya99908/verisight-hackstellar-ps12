# VeriSight model training readiness

**Status (2026-11-11): BLOCKED BEFORE TRAINING — no locally available screenshot corpus or downloadable checkpoint is cleared for this project's intended use.** No trained weights are present. An offline, permission-gated U-Net training scaffold and local loader/inference smoke test have been added; the deployed analyzer remains the deterministic heuristic pipeline. The smoke test uses temporary random test weights and does not report model performance.

## Repository and deployment audit

- `backend/app/services/forensic/pipeline.py` runs ELA/recompression residuals, wavelet statistics, ORB feature matching, OpenCV text-like connected components, metadata/QR inspection, and fixed rule aggregation. It does not recognize visible text with OCR or run an authenticity classifier.
- `backend/app/api/endpoints.py` exposes `/api/model/status` and `/api/model/evaluation`. The live service reports `HEURISTIC_ONLY`, `NOT_CALIBRATED`, `NOT_EVALUATED`, `metrics: null`, five synthetic fixtures, zero independent authentic/manipulated references, and zero held-out cases.
- The frontend label mapper in `frontend/src/services/verdictLabel.ts` retains exactly the requested labels: “Likely accurate” and “Likely misleading.”
- There are no model checkpoints or research datasets in the source checkout. The only local image corpus is the five synthetic regression fixtures under `samples/`; these are not eligible for model training or accuracy claims. A new offline training scaffold requires an explicit permission record, confirmation of source-image rights, authentic captured screenshots and controlled edits in each split, and non-overlapping app/template, device, contributor, original-image, and editing-session IDs.
- `requirements.vercel.txt` contains FastAPI, Pydantic, multipart handling, headless OpenCV, and NumPy; it contains no ML runtime. `vercel.json` allows a 300-second function duration and deployment inspection reports a 77.07 MB FastAPI function. The upload limit is 20 MB. Adding a runtime/model requires cold-start, memory, latency, package-size, and cost measurements before release. Vercel documents Fluid Compute and a large-function beta, but those limits do not remove the need to benchmark the actual packaged model and cold starts: [Python runtime](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/runtimes/python), [large functions](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-functions-can-now-be-up-to-5-gb-in-package-size-7yAwSyCig0IQDXUIDistvS/eadf06d6c3).

## Data collection plan

1. **Define scope and permissions.** List supported Indian payment apps, UPI QR variants, Android/iOS versions, and the intended product/commercial use. Obtain written consent and permission for research, training, validation, retention, and model deployment. Do not collect private chat screenshots without explicit contributor authorization. Store consent and provenance separately from images.
2. **Collect independent authentic examples.** Gather screenshots only with contributor consent and redaction/minimization of account numbers, phone numbers, names, UPI IDs, transaction references, balances, and notifications. Where useful, confirm the payment through the participant's transaction history with their consent; image labels alone do not prove settlement.
3. **Create controlled edits from authentic originals.** For amount, recipient, timestamp, reference, and QR payload, record original-to-edited pairs and masks. Include insertion, removal/inpainting, replacement, splicing, copy-move, full synthetic UI, and AI edits. Keep each original, its edits, crops, recompressions, screen recaptures, and exports linked by a lineage ID.
4. **Collect authentic nuisance transforms.** Apply resizing, JPEG/HEIC conversion, crops, screenshot capture, and messaging-app forwarding to unedited controls as well as edited cases. Record transform recipe, device/app, source template, contributor, edit tool/session, and split group.
5. **Annotate and adjudicate.** Store binary pixel masks, field/region type, edit type, original/edited pair IDs, annotator IDs, adjudication state, app/template/device, and permission record. Have a second reviewer resolve uncertain masks and labels. Remove uncertain labels from primary metrics but track them separately.
6. **Prevent leakage.** Assign groups before augmentation. Keep contributor, payment app/template, original transaction/screenshot, device family, and editing session disjoint across splits where feasible. Hold out at least one app/template and device family for transfer evaluation. Near-duplicate detection must run across splits before training; no augmented derivative may cross a split boundary.
7. **Keep distinct signals distinct.** A text recognizer may extract amount/payee/time/reference values but does not prove they're genuine. Metadata/provenance checks, QR parsing, pixel tamper localization, and provider transaction status must each be reported as separate evidence sources. Only a separately authorized bank/PSP integration can support settlement status.

## Training and evaluation plan after permission/data gate

- The offline implementation is in `backend/app/services/forensic/learned/`; install `backend/requirements-training.txt` only in an isolated research environment. Run it with `PYTHONPATH=backend python -m app.services.forensic.learned.train --manifest <authorized-manifest.json> --data-root <corpus-root> --output <outside-repo>/candidate.pt`. The manifest must explicitly record commercial-use permission, a permission-record path and SHA-256, reviewer and review date, and source-image rights. These are declarations and integrity checks; they do not replace reviewing the actual license/consent terms. It requires authentic and edited labels in each split, complete app/template, device, contributor, original-image, and editing-session group IDs, and rejects exact duplicate images across splits and paths into the bundled `samples/` fixtures. Keep private consent records outside the repository.
- The small U-Net trains against pixel masks with BCE plus Dice loss and applies only training-split resizing/cropping/recompression. Validation loss selects the saved state; the `test` split is not loaded for training or selection. Every produced checkpoint is marked `UNVALIDATED_DO_NOT_DEPLOY`; this scaffold does not compute accuracy, calibrate confidence, or alter the production verdict.
- Loader/inference tests exercise safe `weights_only` checkpoint loading, shape/range checks, and empty/corrupt-image rejection with temporary random test weights. They are software contract tests, not a pretrained model evaluation. A separate CAT-Net checkpoint test remains unavailable because its official host denies download to this account.
- Train an image-level tampering detector with a pixel-level localization head against authorized masks. Treat text localization as the primary task; derive image-level score from a pre-registered mask aggregation rule. Do not call the output a calibrated probability until calibrated on representative validation data.
- Compare at minimum: current heuristics, a transparent classical baseline, and a compact segmentation model. Consider an OCR-assisted branch as a separate ablation. Check any architecture/code/model license independently from the dataset license.
- Apply augmentations only to training data, including resize, JPEG quality changes, app-like recompression, crop, and screen recapture. Apply realistic benign transforms to authentic and manipulated examples alike.
- Select the operating threshold from validation data against a written false-positive target appropriate to intended use. Keep test data locked until model and threshold are frozen.
- Report overall and subgroup false-positive rate, false-negative rate, precision, recall, PR-AUC, image-level calibration/Brier or ECE, pixel IoU, pixel F1, and localization precision/recall. Include counts and confidence intervals by app/template, device, edit type, and compression condition. Compare all metrics to the heuristic baseline.
- Promotion requires: documented rights, no split leakage, a sufficiently represented untouched test set, subgroup reporting, model-load/inference/corrupt-input tests, regression checks, measured production latency/memory/cold start, and a rollback artifact. If a subgroup is underpowered or fails the pre-registered target, expose the limitation and do not claim it is validated for that subgroup.
- Preserve `confidence_score = null` and do not promote to an authenticity classifier if evaluation data, calibration, or generalization remain inadequate. Keep the user-facing labels unchanged.
